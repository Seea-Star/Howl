Welcome to my dedicated shrine for Howl's Moving Castle! Hayao Miyazaki's masterpiece is full of magic, cozy atmospheres, and unforgettable memories. I wanted a special corner on my site to collect all my favorite moments. ✨

Date: Notes      Page: 02

The film is based on the 1986 novel by British author Diana Wynne Jones. The attention to detail in the moving castle's mechanics and the gorgeous pastel backgrounds make it an absolute timeless piece of art. 🪄

Date: Quotes      Page: 03
